# Sample data

## acme/

`handbook.md`, `expenses.md` and `security.md` are policies of Acme Robotics, a fictional
company. They were written for this repository as sample data for the library examples, the
evaluation and the Space, and are covered by the repository's MIT license. Any resemblance to a
real company's policies is coincidental.

## NFL playoffs PDF

`2024–25_NFL_playoffs_pg_1.pdf` is the first page of the Wikipedia article
[2024–25 NFL playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024%E2%80%9325_NFL_playoffs),
printed to PDF on 2025-06-18.
