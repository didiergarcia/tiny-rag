# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

*Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for tiny-rag.*

Acme Robotics designs and builds autonomous robots for warehouses. This handbook covers time off,
leave and where you work.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid vacation per year. After five years of service, this
increases to 23 days per year. Vacation accrues monthly, starting on your first day.

You can carry over up to 5 days of unused vacation into the next calendar year. Carried-over days
expire on March 31 if you have not used them by then.

Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance when you plan to be away for more
than three days in a row.

## Sick leave

Every employee receives 10 sick days per year, separate from vacation. Sick days do not carry over.
A doctor's note is required if you are out sick for more than 3 consecutive days.

## Holidays

The company observes eleven paid holidays. The office is closed from December 24 through
January 1.

## Parental leave

All parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
It can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year after the child arrives.

## Hybrid work

Acme Robotics is a hybrid company. Employees work from the office at least 2 days per week; teams
choose which days. A fully remote arrangement requires approval from your vice president.

Employees who work from home receive a one-time home office stipend of $600 for a desk, chair or
monitor.

## Probation

New employees have a probation period of 90 days. Your manager will schedule a review before it
ends.
