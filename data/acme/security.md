# Acme Robotics Information Security Policy

*Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for tiny-rag.*

These rules protect our customers' data and the software that runs our robots.

## Laptops

Every company laptop uses full-disk encryption. Your screen locks automatically after 5 minutes
without activity. Install software only from the internal software catalog; request anything else
through an IT ticket.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Store them in the company password manager.
Multi-factor authentication is required for every company account, and a hardware security key is
required to access production robot fleets.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT Security within 1 hour, even outside working hours.
Email security@acme-robotics.example or call the security hotline. IT Security will lock and wipe the
device remotely.

## Phishing

Do not click links in unexpected messages. Forward suspicious email to
phishing@acme-robotics.example.

## Customer data

Robot telemetry from customer sites is classified as Confidential. Never copy customer data to a
personal device or personal cloud storage. Telemetry is deleted after two years.

## Visitors

Visitors must wear a badge and be escorted by an employee at all times on the factory floor.

## Training

All employees complete security awareness training within their first two weeks and again every
year.
