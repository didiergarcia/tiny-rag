# Acme Robotics Travel and Expense Policy

*Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This document is sample data for tiny-rag.*

This policy explains what Acme Robotics reimburses and how to claim it.

## Flights

Book all flights through the company travel portal. Fly economy class. For flights longer than
six hours, you may book premium economy.

## Hotels

The hotel limit is $250 per night in most cities. In New York, San Francisco, London and Tokyo, the
limit is $350 per night.

## Meals

While traveling for work, the daily meal limit is $75 per person. Alcohol is not reimbursed unless
you are hosting a customer and your manager approved the dinner in advance.

## Driving

If you drive your own car for work, Acme Robotics reimburses mileage at $0.67 per mile. Parking and
tolls are reimbursed at cost.

## Receipts and deadlines

Attach a receipt for any expense over $25. Submit expense reports within 30 days of the expense.
Approved expenses are paid in the next payroll cycle.

## Approvals

Your manager approves expense reports up to $2,000. Reports above $2,000 also need your department
head. Any single expense above $10,000 must be approved by the chief financial officer (CFO).

## Gifts

Gifts to customers are limited to $100 per person per year.
